# Characters

## Main Characters

### Edward Claude Hamels

Protagonist. Born April 26, 1980. Attends the University of Virginia.

### Emma

Coworker at _Chalk_ who designs the Sasquatch costume in 2006.

### Sadie Trindel

Acquaintance from Washington now living in New York, writing for the _Times_.



## Supporting Cast

+ Kathleen O'Grady: Republican nominee for the Maryland Senate race in 2006 and a self-proclaimed believer in Bigfoot.
